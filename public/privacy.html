QuietLink is designed to shorten URLs without accounts, click analytics, or a personal link dashboard. This page explains what the service needs to do its job.

What we store

When you create a short link, QuietLink stores the destination URL and the expiry timestamp (if you choose one). A random code points to that record. We do not require a name, email address, password, or account.

What we don't track in the application

QuietLink does not count link clicks, build a per-user link history, set analytics cookies, or load third-party advertising or analytics scripts. The redirect code does not record visitor IP addresses, referrers, or click timestamps.

Infrastructure and network information

QuietLink runs on Cloudflare Pages, Functions, and KV. Cloudflare necessarily processes network requests to deliver the service and may handle technical metadata under its own policies and settings. “Privacy-first” does not mean that network traffic is invisible or that the service makes users fully anonymous.

Link expiration and deletion

Links can expire after a selected duration or be set to have no expiry. Expiring records are stored with a database TTL and the redirect handler also checks the expiry timestamp before redirecting. Storage systems may take time to complete physical cleanup, but an expired link should no longer redirect.

“Never expire” means the link remains until it is removed or the service changes; it is not a promise of permanent availability.

Use links carefully

A short URL obscures the original URL in the address you share, but it does not hide the destination from the person who opens it. Only create links you have a right to share. Do not use QuietLink for phishing, malware, impersonation, or unlawful activity. Check destinations before opening unfamiliar links.

Changes and contact

This is the initial privacy notice for the project and may be updated as deployment and abuse-prevention settings evolve. For service issues, use the repository's issue tracker on GitHub.
