ABOUT QUIETLINK

Sharing links,
without the noise.

QuietLink is a simple URL-shortening service built to make long links easier to share while keeping unnecessary data collection out of the application.

What is QuietLink?

QuietLink turns a long web address into a shorter link you can copy and share. Open the short link and QuietLink redirects you to the destination. No account or email address is required to use the service.

Our approach

We aim to keep the service straightforward: random short codes, user-selectable expiration, no click counters, no personal link dashboard, and no third-party analytics scripts in the website.

You're in control of expiration

Choose a link lifetime from one hour up to 365 days, or choose “Never expire.” Expiring links stop redirecting after their expiry time. Links set to never expire remain until removed or the service changes.

Privacy, honestly explained

QuietLink avoids collecting visitor identifiers and click events in its application code. The service runs on Cloudflare, which processes network requests to deliver the site. A short link hides the original address from the shared URL, but it does not make the person opening it fully anonymous or conceal their connection from the destination.

Use links responsibly

Only shorten URLs you have the right to share. Do not use QuietLink for phishing, malware, impersonation, or unlawful activity. Be careful when opening links from people you do not know.
